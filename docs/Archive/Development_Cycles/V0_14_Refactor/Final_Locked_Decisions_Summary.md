# v0.14 Refactor Final Locked-Decisions Summary

> Archived material is non-authoritative for current behavior.

Durable decisions carried forward include: Core remains WPF-free; one validated
provider owns application composition; provider-owned Serilog lifecycle and
startup-only archive retention; workflow-specific result contracts;
`ModPipelineManager` owns admission/cancellation/commit/quiescence; installers
and extractors remain the mechanics authority; incomplete scans preserve the
accepted snapshot; credentials are outside AppConfig; Nexus remains optional and
deferred; and automated tests do not establish manual WPF/Steam/release
acceptance.

Historical alternatives and phase detail remain in the preserved evidence and
are summarized rather than copied into active documentation.
